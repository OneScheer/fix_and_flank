How to play

Each turn: Movement (move your units; a unit that moves cannot fire this turn), Firefight (units that did not move fire), Rally (automatic: suppressed and pinned units roll to recover, sure with the SL in their hex, otherwise the roll must beat the distance to him), Enemy action.

Orders: click a unit, then a hex. Movement: green moves 1 hex, orange fast moves 2 (exposed), a red ring next to your team assaults the enemy in it. Firefight: any hex in the shaded area; a blue ring is a spotted enemy (aimed fire), a red ring next to you an assault, anything else is suppressive fire. Point at a hex to see the dice and odds first. A new order replaces the old one; orders run in the order given; units without an order hold.

Counters: blue box BLUFOR, red diamond OPFOR, letter = team, dots below = soldiers left. Amber badge, one chevron: suppressed (cannot move). Dark red badge, two chevrons, hatched: pinned (cannot move or fire). "!": exposed (fast moved). "?": suspected enemy position. OPFOR shows only once spotted.

Keys: Space commit · Tab next unit · Esc or right click deselect · H hold · V line of sight · drag to pan · wheel to zoom · Home fit the map.
