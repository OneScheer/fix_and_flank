Orders

Your units and their orders

Log


      

      

        Each turn: Movement (move your units; a unit that moves cannot fire this turn), Firefight (units that did not move fire), Rally (automatic: suppressed and pinned units roll to recover, sure with the SL in their hex, otherwise the roll must beat the distance to him), Enemy action.
        Left click a unit to select it, then left click a hex. Movement: green moves 1 hex, orange fast moves 2 (exposed), a red ring next to your team assaults the enemy in it. Firefight: any hex in the red area; a red ring is a spotted enemy (aimed fire; in the next hex, an assault), anything else is suppressive fire. Point at a hex to see the dice and odds first.
        Right click or Esc deselects. Clicking a hex with a team and the SL again switches between them.
        A new order replaces the old one. Orders run in the order given. Units without an order hold.
        H hold (remove the selected unit's order), Tab next unit, Space commits the phase. V shades what the selected unit cannot see. Drag to pan, wheel to zoom, Home fits the map.
        OPFOR is only shown once spotted (in the open, adjacent, moving fast, or firing); ? marks a suspected position.
